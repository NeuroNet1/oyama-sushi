## 🎨 Visual Content Strategy

### Photography Guidelines
- **Lighting**: Natural light, warm tones
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, close-ups
- **Styling**: Minimalist, authentic Japanese aesthetic
- **Colors**: Rich reds, deep greens, natural wood tones

### Video Content Ideas
1. **Chef's Table Series**: Behind-the-scenes sushi making
2. **Dish Preparation**: Time-lapse of signature dishes
3. **Customer Testimonials**: Real reviews on video
4. **Virtual Tour**: 360° restaurant walkthrough
5. **Cooking Tips**: Quick Japanese cooking lessons

### Instagram Strategy
- **Daily Posts**: Food photography, chef moments
- **Stories**: Behind-the-scenes, daily specials

3. **Video**: Turn into YouTube video
4. **Infographic**: Visual summary
5. **Podcast**: Audio version (future)
